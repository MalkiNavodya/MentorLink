1. Introduction

Before developing MentorLink, it is important to understand the people who will use the platform. Every software application is created to solve problems for its users, and understanding those users helps developers design a better system.

MentorLink is built for university students who want to learn new skills or share their knowledge with others. Some students will use the platform to find mentors, while others will join as mentors to teach and guide fellow students. The system will also have an administrator who manages the platform and ensures that everything operates smoothly.

The following user personas represent the main types of users who are expected to interact with MentorLink.



2. Persona 1 – The Learner

Name: Nethmi Perera

Age: 21

University: University of Kelaniya

Degree Program: BSc in Information Technology

Academic Year: Second Year

About

Nethmi is an enthusiastic IT undergraduate who enjoys learning new technologies. She wants to improve her Laravel and Angular knowledge before applying for internships. Although she often asks questions in university WhatsApp groups, she finds it difficult to identify students who have the right expertise. She prefers a dedicated platform where she can easily find experienced mentors.

Goals

• Learn modern web development technologies.

• Prepare for software engineering internships.

• Improve practical programming skills.

• Connect with experienced university students.

Challenges

• Finding the right person to ask for help.

• Receiving inconsistent advice from different sources.

• Learning without proper guidance.

How MentorLink Helps

MentorLink allows Nethmi to search for mentors based on specific skills, view mentor profiles, send mentoring requests, communicate with mentors, and receive guidance that supports her learning journey.



3. Persona 2 – The Mentor

Name: Kavindu Fernando

Age: 24

University: University of Colombo

Degree Program: BSc in Computer Science

Academic Year: Fourth Year

About

Kavindu enjoys teaching junior students and believes that sharing knowledge strengthens the university community. He has experience with Laravel, Angular, and database development. Instead of answering the same questions repeatedly through personal messages, he wants a platform that organizes mentoring requests efficiently.

Goals

• Help junior students.

• Improve communication and leadership skills.

• Build a strong professional reputation.

• Expand his professional network.

Challenges

• Managing multiple mentoring requests.

• Organizing mentoring sessions.

• Reaching students who genuinely need help.

How MentorLink Helps

MentorLink enables Kavindu to create a mentor profile, showcase his technical skills, manage mentoring requests, schedule sessions, and receive ratings from learners.



4. Persona 3 – The Busy Student

Name: Sanduni Jayasinghe

Age: 22

University: SLIIT

Degree Program: BSc in Information Technology

Academic Year: Third Year

About

Sanduni manages university studies alongside a part-time job. Because of her busy schedule, she needs a platform that helps her quickly find suitable mentors without spending hours searching through social media groups.

Goals

• Save time.

• Improve programming knowledge.

• Prepare for industrial training.

• Learn efficiently.

Challenges

• Limited free time.

• Difficulty finding reliable mentors.

• Managing study and work responsibilities.

How MentorLink Helps

MentorLink provides search filters that allow Sanduni to quickly find mentors based on skills and availability, making learning more convenient.



5. Persona 4 – The New University Student

Name: Tharindu Silva

Age: 19

University: NSBM Green University

Degree Program: BSc in Software Engineering

Academic Year: First Year

About

Tharindu recently started university and feels overwhelmed by the number of programming languages and technologies he needs to learn. He wants guidance from senior students who have already experienced university life and industry preparation.

Goals

• Learn programming fundamentals.

• Build confidence.

• Meet experienced students.

• Prepare for future career opportunities.

Challenges

• Lack of practical experience.

• Limited connections with senior students.

• Uncertainty about where to begin.

How MentorLink Helps

MentorLink introduces Tharindu to experienced mentors who can guide him through his university journey and help him build confidence step by step.



6. Persona 5 – System Administrator

Role: System Administrator

About

The administrator is responsible for managing MentorLink and ensuring that the platform remains secure, organized, and user-friendly. Unlike students, administrators focus on maintaining the system rather than participating in mentoring sessions.

Responsibilities

• Manage user accounts.

• Manage skill categories.

• Review reported content.

• Remove inappropriate information.

• Monitor platform performance.

• Maintain security and data integrity.

Goals

• Ensure a safe learning environment.

• Maintain system reliability.

• Improve user satisfaction.

• Resolve technical and administrative issues promptly.



7. Conclusion

The user personas presented in this document represent the primary users of MentorLink and help define the purpose of the platform. By understanding each user's goals, challenges, and expectations, development decisions can focus on solving real problems rather than simply adding features.

Throughout the development process, every new feature should support at least one of these personas and contribute to creating a user-friendly, engaging, and effective mentorship platform for university students.
